# Prose

The full guide is `~/projects/SMB_style_guidelines/style_guide.md`. News posts
add `docs/news-style.md`.

W1. No em dashes, no semicolons, no colons joining clauses. Split into
    sentences. Labels ("Advisor:") and official titles are fine.
W2. Greek letters as inline math (`$\nu_\tau$`, `$p\gamma$`), not spelled out.
W3. Active voice, subject first. "we" for joint work, "I" only for single-author work.
W4. American spelling. Short sentences, under ~25 words.
W5. Never "my student". Name the person with the role in parentheses:
    "Ho Man Yim (PhD candidate)", "Ruohan Li (PhD)" once graduated.
W6. Low-key. One headline number, no hype words ("novel", "groundbreaking"),
    no self-promotional metric tiles.
W7. Use only facts from the source. Never invent a number, a comparison or an experiment's adoption.
W8. Light markup only: `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `[text](url)`, `$...$`. It must
    render in both HTML and LaTeX.
