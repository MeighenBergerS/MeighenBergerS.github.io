The Sun is too hot to keep light dark matter, which evaporates before it can annihilate into neutrinos. With Sandra Robles, we show that Jupiter, with its much cooler core, holds on to dark matter lighter than a few GeV. Hyper-Kamiokande could then see the resulting neutrinos and reach interaction strengths of $2\times10^{-35}$ cm$^2$ below 2 GeV. That surpasses current limits from the Sun and from direct detection in this mass range.
