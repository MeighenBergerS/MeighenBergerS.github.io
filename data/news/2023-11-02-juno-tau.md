At low energies, tau neutrinos ($\nu_\tau$) from atmospheric oscillations cannot make tau leptons, so nobody has seen them appear. With John Beacom, Nicole Bell and Matthew Dolan, we propose a way to detect them in JUNO, through neutrinos of any flavor knocking neutrons out of carbon. With oscillation into $\nu_\tau$, JUNO should see 55 such events per year, instead of 41 if the muon neutrinos simply disappeared. With better laboratory cross-section data, JUNO can reach $5\sigma$ sensitivity within five years.
