Reactor experiments led by Daya Bay measured the neutrino mixing angle $\theta_{13}$ to about 3 percent, and no planned experiment is expected to do better. With Matheus Hostert and Maxim Pospelov, we asked what a small reactor could do if it were moved between two spots near the giant JUNO detector in China. We find that a 20-megawatt reactor in JUNO's access tunnel could measure $\theta_{13}$ three to four times better than JUNO alone, reaching about 4 percent over a decade.
