Machine learning now reconstructs neutrino events well inside individual telescopes, but comparing methods across experiments has been nearly impossible without public data. With colleagues, we built NuBench, an open benchmark of almost 130 million simulated neutrino interactions in six detector layouts, generated with Prometheus. We test four reconstruction algorithms, including those used in KM3NeT and IceCube. My part was the simulation code and the public data sets.
