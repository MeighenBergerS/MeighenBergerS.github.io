Satellites and balloons measure cosmic rays directly, but only at specific places and times, and their results do not fully agree. With Jayden Newstead and Louis Strigari, we show that detectors such as Hyper-Kamiokande can measure the cosmic rays through the atmospheric neutrinos they produce. Our technique reduces the uncertainty on the neutrino flux from about 20% to about 7%. That in turn improves Hyper-K's sensitivity to the mixing angle $\theta_{23}$ by a factor of 2.
