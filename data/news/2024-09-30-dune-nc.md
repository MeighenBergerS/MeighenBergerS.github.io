Comparing electron neutrinos with neutrinos of all flavors is how the SNO experiment proved that solar neutrinos oscillate. With Jayden Newstead, John Beacom, Nicole Bell and Matthew Dolan, we show that DUNE can measure all flavors through neutral-current interactions that excite argon nuclei. Combined with DUNE's other solar channels, this would give the most precise measurements of the solar mixing parameters using solar neutrinos. It requires dedicated, but realistic, work on DUNE's low-energy capabilities and on neutrino-argon cross sections.
